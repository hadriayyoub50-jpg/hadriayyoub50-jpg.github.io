03 / THE STORY

WHY
AVENIGMA?

Music started as a way to put difficult experiences somewhere outside the mind. In 2021, that need became a creative direction.

The name AVENIGMA brings together two ideas: IV, representing Mr. IV and the “ultra evil,” and Enigma, representing the poet. Together, they reflect a life that can feel impossible to explain — so the explanation becomes music.

The goal isn't to make everything easy to understand. It's to make people feel it, question it, and eventually comprehend it through words.
